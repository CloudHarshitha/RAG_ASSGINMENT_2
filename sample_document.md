# Acme Corp - Remote Work and Equipment Policy

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time employees at Acme Corp are eligible for up to 3 days of remote work per week after completing their first 90 days of employment. 
Employees must coordinate their remote days with their direct supervisor to ensure adequate team coverage in the office. 

## 2. Core Hours
Regardless of working remotely or in the office, all employees must be online and available for meetings during our "Core Hours" which are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (EST), Monday through Friday.

## 3. Home Office Equipment Setup
Acme Corp provides a one-time stipend of $500 for home office setup. This can be used for purchasing a desk, an ergonomic chair, or additional monitors. Receipts must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 30 days of purchase.

### Company Provided Equipment
The company will directly provide the following to every employee:
* One 14-inch laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS)
* One wireless mouse
* One noise-canceling headset (Jabra Evolve2)

## 4. Internet Reimbursement
Employees working remotely at least 2 days a week can expense up to $50 per month towards their home internet bill.

## 5. Security Protocols
When working from home, employees must connect to the Acme Corp VPN before accessing any internal company portals or databases. 
Public Wi-Fi networks (such as in coffee shops) are strictly prohibited unless the VPN is actively engaged.

## 6. Communication Expectations
Employees must update their Slack status to indicate their working location (e.g., 🏠 for Home, 🏢 for Office). 
Response time expectations remain the same regardless of location:
* Slack messages during working hours: Within 2 hours
* Emails: Within 24 hours (1 business day)

## 7. Sick Days
If you are unwell, please take a sick day rather than attempting to work remotely. Notify your manager and HR as soon as possible. Remote work is not a substitute for resting and recovering from illness.
